# M54 — xmom entry geometry: arm against the EXPECTED fill price (correctness / fee-bleed, NOT edge)

**Status:** DONE. **Branch:** `feat/m54-xmom-entry-geometry-expected-fill`. **Plan:**
[`docs/plans/M54-xmom-entry-geometry-expected-fill.md`](../../plans/M54-xmom-entry-geometry-expected-fill.md).

## What this milestone is

A surgical correctness fix to *where* xmom arms its stop-loss and take-profit at signal time. Before
M54, geometry was anchored to the signal 5m close (`entryPrice = latestBar.close`), but the ADR 0045
fill-acceptance guard measures realized R:R against the actual fill. On thin books a long
systematically fills above signal (adverse), so the guard sees a realized R:R biased below the arm
ratio and `force_close`s the fill at 0-duration. M54 arms the SL/TP against an **expected fill
price** — `signal + modeled adverse slippage` (half-spread) — so the geometry is honest relative to
where the order will actually fill, and predictably-degenerate thin-book candidates are skipped
before any order is sent.

**This is NOT an edge fix.** EXP-021 (fragile peak, 1/10 folds) and EXP-022 (all 22 exit-geometry
variants net-negative; the binding constraint is the signal, not exit mechanics) already proved xmom
has no cost-surviving edge and that widening geometry does not create one. Success is measured as
force_close-rate reduction and fee/slot-churn reduction — never PnL improvement.

## What shipped

- **D1 (`packages/shared/src/schema/momentumParamsSchema.ts`):** two new params,
  `xmom_expected_fill_enabled` (boolean, default `false`) and `xmom_max_depth_fraction`
  (number|null, default `null`), with a Zod `.superRefine` rejecting `enabled=true` with
  `max_depth_fraction` null/undefined/non-finite. Both default to a byte-identical no-op.
- **D2 (`apps/engine/src/strategy/service/MomentumOrchestratorService.ts`):**
  `buildMomentumOpenIntent` arms SL/TP off an expected-fill price `F_exp = P0 × (1 +
  halfSpread/100)` instead of the raw signal price `P0`, via a new private
  `resolveExpectedFillPrice`. Adds a pre-send, order-size-aware depth-budget skip
  (`isDepthBudgetExceeded`) that fails closed on null/≤0 book depth. `referencePrice` /
  `midAtTrigger` / `entryPrice` on the emitted intent stay anchored to `P0` (protects the M48
  `slFloor` and M52 `atrUnitsDrift` breaker). Log-only observability
  (`MOMENTUM_EXPECTED_FILL_ANCHOR`, `MOMENTUM_DEPTH_SKIP`). The M52 retry rebuild inherits both
  the anchor and the skip automatically since it reuses the same builder.
- **D3 (`packages/analysis/research/xmom_tp_ratio_replay.mjs`):** extended with `--expected-fill`
  and `--max-depth-fraction` flags; re-anchors recorded geometry to `F_exp` off the real recorded
  fill (fill-anchor-invariant); reports decision-grade (force_close-rate, skip count, fee-churn)
  and characterization (SL-hit-rate, PnL delta) metrics separately, the latter explicitly labeled
  non-comparable to the EXP-021/022 full-universe baseline. Registered as **EXP-025** (not EXP-023
  as the original plan draft assumed — EXP-023 and EXP-024 were already allocated to other work on
  2026-07-08 by the time this ran). EXP-025 doc:
  `docs/analysis/20260709-xmom-expected-fill-anchor-exp025.md`, a proportionate stub with results
  PENDING the actual paper-soak run.
- **D4:** 29 adversarial tests across 3 new files (`MomentumOrchestratorService.m54.spec.ts`,
  `MomentumOrchestratorService.m54.retry.spec.ts`, `exitGeometryHelper.m54.spec.ts`) covering all
  11 items from plan §9. Also fixed a fixture gap in 2 pre-existing spec files

  that hand-built params bypassing the schema parse — not a production bug, just missing the two

## ADR context

- **ADR 0047** (portfolio-strategy contract) — **amended**, new §7 (sibling to the M53 §6
  amendment). Documents the two new params, their no-op defaults, the expected-fill anchor
  decision (arm off `F_exp`, keep `referencePrice = P0`), the half-spread model and why not the
  backtest's flat-slippage model (EXP-008), the depth-fraction skip, and the
  "correctness/fee-bleed, NOT edge" scope framing (EXP-021/022).
- **ADR 0045** (fill-acceptance guard) — **referenced, not amended**. The guard is byte-for-byte
  unchanged; a note records that the arm now anchors to the expected fill, so the guard's realized
  R:R is centered at the floor instead of biased below it.
- **ADR 0051** (M52 force_close retry) — **referenced, not amended**. A note records that the
  expected-fill anchor + skip reduce the force_close arrival rate the retry mechanism responds to,
  and that the retry rebuild inherits the anchor+skip via the shared builder, but the retry
  contract itself and `atrUnitsDrift`/`referencePrice` are unchanged.
- **ADR 0004 §6a** (`isBookTooThin` per-coin depth eligibility) — **referenced, not amended**.
  Records the two-layer reconciliation: M54's pre-gate, order-size-aware slippage-budget skip sits
  above the unchanged in-gate static tier-floor guard, adopts its fail-closed convention, and does
  not touch the M51/ADR 0042 §9 paper-relax.
